# deep-canvas-physics

## What it is for

The physics canvas module (issue #2009, `physics.world@1`, measured, with backend_bytes state): the world document's reader with the design's defaults, the caps a backend declares and the check that refuses what they rule out, naming the field and the backend, and the backend interface a backend implements. Everything here is this crate's own types, so no backend type crosses the interface. Issue #2010 adds the bake key, the runtime digest and the outputs sampled at the document's frame rate (`src/bake.rs`), and step hashes, the golden comparator and where a golden stands across machines (`src/golden.rs`). Issue #2011 adds roles that expand through a first-party table into collision groups (`src/roles.rs`), the check kind `physics.pairs` over the whole interaction matrix (`src/pairs.rs`), inline `convex`, `mesh` and `compound` shapes, `budget.step_p95`, and sensor entries and exits in the backend interface's `occurrences`. Issue #1918 adds the occurrence list, made canonical and sorted from what a backend reports (`src/occurrence.rs`), the module that fills the core's `column.source@1` with `<world ID>.occurrences` and `<world ID>.<ID>.<read>` (`src/module.rs`), and the reader's kinematic bodies, `follow`, channel motor targets, `breaks_at`, `tag`, `solve` and outputs (`src/doc/reads.rs`). Issue #522 adds a world's declared inputs and `checkpoint.budget` (`src/doc/inputs.rs`), its input track, ordered by (kind, target ID) whoever wrote it (`src/track.rs`), and the byte-budgeted checkpoint ring whose spacing doubles when it would pass its budget (`src/ring.rs`). Issue #523 adds a joint's own state beside its bodies' in the interface and the step hash (a hinge's whole turns and its steps past `breaks_at`), `joint_coordinate` and `joint_force` outputs (`{force, torque}`, its locks' and limits' impulses, never its motor's) sampled into the bake, an output's `crossings`, the `fault` (`speed_cap`, `jam`) and `crossing` rows, gear and pulley joints, and `physics.mass_ratio` (`src/check.rs`). Issue #524 adds `soft@1` ropes, cloth, balloons and soft volumes, each a lattice of particles with stable addresses (`src/doc/soft.rs`, with the world's `capacity`), particles in the canonical state and step hash after the joints, `tear` rows, the backend's read-back of what it built (`held`), and skins: a soft body drawn on an item built on its own lattice, refused at check otherwise (`src/skin.rs`). Issue #191 adds the interface's behavior part (`src/kit.rs`, `src/kit/`): its fixtures as data, each a world, a measure, an analytic answer, a tolerance and the caps it needs, run on any backend through the backend interface only, with the query fixture's brute-force scan (`src/kit/scan.rs`) and the sensor matrix (`src/kit/sensors.rs`); the caps' `claims`, which the check refuses a world past; the input kind `deactivate`; and the lint `physics.fast_body` with the `ccd` default it expands (`src/fast.rs`). Issue #2239 adds the caps' `sweep`, what the backend's swept collision holds as data, which the check reads to refuse `ccd` on a kinematic or a static body and the lint reads to count a body's fastest point, to raise kind `kinematic` for a fast body no sweep starts from and kind `pair` for two bodies that neither a sweep nor a `ccd` body's own contact prediction holds, each with a fix that settles it, and to leave a body another's sweep holds without the `ccd` fix. Issue #2308 adds `solver.length_unit`, the length a backend scales its own lengths by, refused out of its range and, by the caps' `scales_lengths`, on a backend that scales none; the lint `physics.small_body`, run at check, with its kinds `unit` and `soft` (`src/small.rs`); and the kit's small fixtures at 480 Hz with contacts at 240 Hz and a solver length of 0.01 m: the stack, the bounce and the slide of 1 cm bodies, a cube off a table's edge and a row of dominoes (`src/kit/small.rs`). Issue #1931 adds three closed force laws, `inverse_square`, `magnetic` and `lennard_jones` (`src/doc/laws.rs`), each claimed by name in a backend's caps: a world holding the first or the last runs at `substeps: 1`, the last takes at most 256 bodies, and the kit holds an orbit, a gyration and a pair's rest distance to their worked answers (`src/kit/laws.rs`). Issue #2317 adds the kit's turntable, a box that a heavy disc on a motor's hinge carries round, with a fixture's `ramps`, the rise of a motor's target that the kit hands a backend at each step as a scene's channel would (`src/kit/turns.rs`); a body's thinnest extent where the lint names it of kind `unit`, which a backend reads (`src/small.rs`); and the caps' sentence on which pairs keep their contact points.

## Where new code goes

- A world, shape, body, joint or force field goes in `src/doc.rs`, with its default and range; a closed force law's fields, and the rules a world holding one is held to, in `src/doc/laws.rs`; #1918's fields and the outputs go in `src/doc/reads.rs`; an input kind or field, or a checkpoint field, in `src/doc/inputs.rs`.
- What a world's `dimensions` changes in its reading (a 2D world's points, turns, locks and shape kinds, and what it refuses until its backend runs it) and a body's `inertia` go in `src/doc/dims.rs`; a wind's fields in `src/doc/wind.rs` and what it blows, in pinned math, in `src/wind.rs` (issue #2098).
- A `soft@1` field, an address pattern or a soft lattice goes in `src/doc/soft.rs`; what a skin checks, bakes and writes into a frame in `src/skin.rs`.
- How a track's entries resolve against the declared inputs, order, fork and join, and the entries a control's entries derive (`from_controls`, which a world's bake, a live play's world and deep-show share), go in `src/track.rs`; the ring's spacing and budget in `src/ring.rs`.
- The occurrence list's kinds, canonical order, bytes and rows go in `src/occurrence.rs`; the manifest and the `column.source@1` entries in `src/module.rs`.
- What a backend can't run goes in `src/caps.rs` as data, and its refusal in `src/check.rs`.
- The backend interface is `src/backend.rs`; a backend lives in its own crate (Rapier's is `crates/deep-physics-rapier`).
- A part of the bake key or how outputs are sampled goes in `src/bake.rs`; a hash, the comparator or a golden's cross-machine verdict in `src/golden.rs`. What the build resolved reaches them as a `Build` the backend's crate passes in. A golden's runtime digest is the seal's, of the set that holds this module alone (`Build::runtime_digest`, #1920).
- What an output's value is after a step (a read, a fold of particles or an `expr`), and its series in a bake, rows for one a series samples (#2248), go in `src/reading.rs`; what an output writes into its scene's items, a readout's value among them, in `src/written.rs`.
- A role's groups go in `TABLE` in `src/roles.rs`; a rule a role promises, which physics.pairs holds whatever the table says, goes in `src/pairs.rs`.
- The codes it adds to the check catalog are `CODES` in `src/check.rs`.
- The acceptance tests are in `tests/acceptance/2009`, `2010`, `2011`, `1918`, `1920`, `522`, `524`, `191`, `2098` and `1931`.

## What it may import

A canvas module crate under `scripts/canvas-lint.mjs`. It reaches the core through `deep-canvas` by crate name and reads JSON with `serde_json`. It depends on no physics engine and does no float math beyond comparisons.

## Deviations

- `src/module.rs`'s manifest names the backend's crate as `physics@1`'s implementation, measured with backend_bytes state, with the pins the build resolved (#1920). Its behavior part is `src/kit.rs`, which deep-show runs as physics@1's kit part `behavior` (#191). It registers no operator or kind: the host bakes a scene's worlds at check, as the logic module's items are baked. Its conformance-kit stepper and fixture need an engine, so deep-show registers them beside it (#522).
- `src/backend.rs` declares `id`, `caps`, `build`, `step`, `occurrences`, `read`, `canonical`, `held` (#524), `checkpoint` and `restore`; `query`, `move_character` and `drive_vehicle` join it with #1919, #182 and #251.
- `src/doc.rs` refuses by name, with the issue that brings each, the fields no criterion of #2009, #2010, #2011 or #1918 runs: character and vehicle bodies, heightfield shapes and shapes built `from` an item or asset (#927), swing and twist, the buoyancy force, an `inverse_square` point read from a channel (in `src/doc/laws.rs`, which no criterion of #1931 runs), `jump` (#182), `drive`, `steer` and `brake` (#251), `launch.from` and expressions in an input's `data` (#164), and character reads; `src/check.rs` refuses a soft body's `pressure` by the caps, and rack and path joints in kinematic mode, which no criterion of #523 runs.
- `src/bake.rs` keys the scene's channels and drivers a world reads as the values they give at every step (`reads`), and the input track as its entries resolve, defaults merged, so a track written with or without its defaults keys the same bake.
- `src/doc/inputs.rs` reads a grab's `plane` and keeps it, but no `move` here reads it: a move names the point it pulls to.
- `src/doc/dims.rs` refuses in a 2D world, each naming #2098's follow-up, what no criterion of #2098 runs there: joints, forces, inputs, outputs, sensors, kinematic bodies, kinematic mode, soft bodies and a body's `inertia`. `src/wind.rs` pushes the faces of a body's boxes only, and refuses a wind on a dynamic body with another shape or on a vehicle body; no wind names a soft body yet.
- #2371 reads the fields the design leaves untyped this way. A `set` targets a force and its `path` is `strength`, the one field the design marks settable; the force names that input by ID as its `strength` and starts at the input's `value`. A drag's `medium` and a spring's `point` written as a string are an item path of the world's scene, as `follow` is. A blast entry reaches its own target, so an input's `targets` say which bodies a blast may move. `falloff` is metres past `radius` over which the impulse falls linearly to nothing, 0 by default, measured to a shape's nearest point; a poke with a `radius` reaches its target the same way from its point. `impulse_per_length` multiplies the width of each shape reached, the diameter of its bounding sphere. A poke's `address` is `<body>#<anchor>`, an anchor of any body of the world, refused at check when none has it. `src/track.rs` drops a `set` when a later-written one of the same step, target and path joins the track.
- `src/occurrence.rs`'s rows carry no time: a host stamps each with its step's time (`timed`) where a scene's readers read it.
- `src/doc/soft.rs` builds a balloon's and a volume's lattice only from the `mesh` a host resolves (deep-physics-rapier's `source.rs`), since this crate builds no mesh; a volume from a mesh asset is built there too (#2457), from the asset's bytes its host keeps in the scene, since no world reads a store. A soft body's `collision_group` makes it a member of that group, colliding with all; its clusters are named address sets, not engine clusters.
- `src/skin.rs` writes a skin's positions into its item each frame, which scene3d rebuilds on the CPU (a new GPU buffer a frame), not the design's in-place vertex buffer rewrite.
- `src/doc.rs` reads only `step_p95` of `budget`; `memory` and `bake_10s` are accepted and not yet held (benchmarks/physics.mjs measures them for #524's budgets). The hosts block (`host@1`) that will say which hosts have a reference Mac doesn't exist yet, so `scripts/physics-budget.mjs` takes it in its request.
