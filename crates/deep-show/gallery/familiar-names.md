# Familiar names

Agents who know another tool's words can ask deep_kinds for one word at a time. A familiar-names pack maps each word it knows onto a kind, with the conversion the pack states, such as a radius becoming r in u. A word no installed pack maps answers no_match: deep_kinds never guesses a mapping from spelling.

An unknown kind or field in a document is refused with up to three close names. A name a rename record gives comes first, with the kind version that renamed it.
