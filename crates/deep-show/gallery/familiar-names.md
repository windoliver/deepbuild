# Familiar names

Agents who know another tool's words can ask deep_kinds for one word at a time. A familiar-names pack maps each word it knows onto a kind, with the conversion the pack states, such as a radius becoming r in u. A word no installed pack maps answers no_match: deep_kinds never guesses a mapping from spelling.

Each such pack keeps a census of its reference's public names, each mapped, refused with a reason, or open, and deep_kinds answers a word with its state. familiar@1 maps 22 of 444 names: every name the web 3D library's WebGL entry exports at 0.186.0, and its rounded-box addon.

An unknown kind or field in a document is refused with up to three close names. A name a rename record gives comes first, with the kind version that renamed it, then a kind that defines the field, then an installed pack the document doesn't name whose census maps or refuses the word, then names by spelling. A pack's word is never accepted in place of its target: name the pack in the document's packs and write the target.
