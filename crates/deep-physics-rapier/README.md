# deep-physics-rapier

## What it is for

physics.world@1's backend (issue #2009): Rapier 0.36.1, pinned with enhanced-determinism and serde-serialize and without simd8 or parallel, behind `deep-canvas-physics`'s backend interface. A world is built in canonical order into one `PhysicsWorld` and stepped once a step, the world's substeps being Rapier's solver iterations. A deep checkpoint is that world's own bytes, stamped with the backend and version that wrote them, and restore refuses another version's, naming both. The `deep-physics` binary runs worlds over JSON lines for the acceptance tests. Issue #2010 adds what the build resolved (`build.rs`), keyed bakes kept in a store, playback, goldens and the floating-point control register's guard (`src/fp.rs`). Issue #2011 adds convex, mesh and compound colliders, the narrow-phase tests each pair of shape uses ran in a step, and the runner's `pairs`, `time`, `time_build` and `time_end` calls, which `scripts/physics-budget.mjs` uses to hold `budget.step_p95` natively and in headless Chrome. Issue #1918 adds each step's occurrences, read from the world after the step (`src/occurrences.rs`): contacts, hits, sensor entries and exits tested on the step's poses, rest and wake, and breaks; kinematic bodies, channel motor targets, a kinematic solve and breaking joints; and a world inside the scene that lists it (`src/scene.rs`). Issue #522 adds the inputs a step applies at its start (`src/inputs.rs`: launch, poke, grab, move and release, a held grab riding in the checkpoint after the world's bytes), the checkpoint ring in every bake, the runner's `seek` and `determinism` (`src/runner/timeline.rs`), its live and baked views (`src/runner/live.rs`: `view`, `frame` and `record`), and the planted restores (`src/planted.rs`). Issue #523 adds each joint's own state and readings (`src/joints.rs`): a hinge's whole turns, kept with its steps past `breaks_at` in the joint's `user_data` so a checkpoint carries them, its force from its locks' and limits' impulses only, `fault {kind: speed_cap}` for each cap a step meets and an output's crossings; and the kinematic solve (`src/kinematic.rs`), which places each body a hinge, slider or fixed joint carries and solves the undriven coordinates so gears, pulleys and closing joints hold, writing `fault {kind: jam}` when its iteration cap ends past its tolerance. Issue #524 runs `soft@1` on Rapier 0.36.1's soft bodies (`src/soft.rs`, the one file that names an engine soft-body type), each particle followed through tears by name and its torn lattice edges written as `tear` rows; a balloon's and a soft volume's meshes resolved from scene3d where the world meets its scene (`src/source.rs`); `check`'s read-back of what the engine built (`src/runner/held.rs`); physics.audit at its defaults (`src/runner/audit.rs`); and the pose channels an output to an item path writes, shared by deep-show and deep-gpu (`src/channels.rs`). Issue #191 adds the runner's `backend`, `behavior_fixtures`, `behavior_kit` and `expand` calls (`src/runner/kit.rs`), sensors that see kinematic bodies, `deactivate`, swept collision with predicted contacts for a body with `ccd`, the lint `physics.fast_body` in every bake's `issues`, and, in a `test-backends` build, `--backend <name>`: Rapier wrong one way under caps of its own. Issue #2239 has `expand` take a scene's world, write a row only for a fast dynamic body and check the world it writes, and adds its planted programs (`src/runner/fast.rs`). Issue #2457 builds a soft volume from a mesh asset, its particles the welded vertices of the asset's glTF node as the item is placed (`src/source.rs`): a world reads no store, so a call on a scene's world takes `evidence`, an evidence store folder its mesh assets are read from by SHA-256, and a host that calls in process keeps them in the scene it hands over (`source::sourced`); an asset neither holds is refused `asset_missing`. Issue #2310 builds an `articulated` joint as a link of Rapier's multibody (`src/articulated.rs`): its coordinates are set from its two bodies' authored poses, a pair written more than 1 mm apart is refused, its `joint_coordinate` is the engine's own coordinate, and its planted programs and the test backend `no_articulations` are in `src/runner/links.rs` and `src/runner/kit.rs`. Issue #2308 sets Rapier's `length_unit` from the world's `solver.length_unit`, keeps `solver.max_speed` and `solver.sleep.speed` in m/s whatever it is, raises `physics.small_body` in `check`'s `issues`, and adds its planted programs (`src/runner/contact.rs`) and, in a `test-backends` build, a backend whose caps say it scales no lengths (`src/runner/kit.rs`). Issue #2371 adds the inputs `set`, `activate` and `blast`, a `deactivate` that also puts its body to sleep, a poke's `address`, `radius` and `falloff` (`src/inputs.rs`, the strengths inputs set riding in the checkpoint before the held grabs), a drag's medium and a spring's point read from the scene at each step's time (`src/scene.rs`), and the planted track `set_applies_both`. Issue #1931 adds the three closed force laws (`src/laws.rs`): an inverse square about a point, a magnetic turn made by the half-angle rotation, so it never changes a speed, and a Lennard-Jones pair summed in canonical body order, with their planted programs and the test backend that claims them (`src/runner/laws.rs`). Issue #2317 has the contacts of a body on one that turns found from the two poses at every step (`src/belt.rs`): Rapier keeps a pair's contact points, fixed in the world, while its bodies have moved less than a twentieth of its length against each other, and never for a pair under a contact hook, so the wrapper's hook is active, carrying nothing, on every shape use of a kinematic body and of a dynamic body a hinge's motor turns, and on a dynamic body thinner than that distance for the one step after it has moved a twentieth of its own thinnest extent from where its contacts were last found, a place its body's `user_data` carries through a checkpoint; its two planted wrappers leave the reuse as Rapier has it and turn it off for every world (`src/runner/contact.rs`). Issue #3258 has physics.audit read `soft.through` with `deep_canvas_physics::through` at every step, its planted readings in `src/runner/through.rs`.

## Where new code goes

- Building, stepping and reading a world, and checkpoints, go in `src/lib.rs`; applying an input in `src/inputs.rs`; anything that names an engine soft-body type in `src/soft.rs` alone (the `test-swap-soft-solver` build swaps that module's solver for a small one of ours, #524 criterion 3).
- An articulated joint goes in `src/articulated.rs` (issue #2310): the one file that names an engine multibody type but the planted restore in `src/planted.rs`; its coordinates live in the world's bytes, so a checkpoint carries them.
- A closed force law's push goes in `src/laws.rs` (issue #1931), computed once per step from the state at t(k) beside the other forces in `src/forces.rs`'s `push`, which the step holds over its substeps; a law takes the force's `strength` itself, since a magnetic turn scales its field.
- A belt's contact hook goes in `src/belt.rs` (issue #2241): a contact material's `tangent_speed` rides in its collider's `user_data`, so the world's bytes carry it, and the hook gives each of its contacts that surface velocity along the shape use's own +X.
- A `dimensions: 2` world on rapier2d goes in `src/dim2.rs` (issue #2098): `Rapier` builds one there and answers the backend interface from it, and `src/runner/dims.rs` picks the caps a world is held to and the build its key names.
- What the build resolved goes in `build.rs`, read by `build()` in `src/lib.rs`, and by `build_2d()` for a 2D world's backend and the crates under it.
- The physics module's manifest is deep-canvas-physics's (`module::module`), built from `build()`: deep-show's full build links this crate for it, so the plan it publishes and a golden recorded here name the same implementation and pins (issue #1920).
- The control register's guard goes in `src/fp.rs`.
- What a step wrote (contacts, hits, sensors, rest and wake, breaks) goes in `src/occurrences.rs`; it only reads the world, so a world's bytes and checkpoints are what they were. The `test-reverse-pairs` feature builds #1918's test backend, which reads every pair last to first from its second collider's side.
- What a world reads of its scene (paths, the cycle check, the key's reads) goes in `src/scene.rs`.
- The JSON-lines runner goes in `src/runner.rs`, shared by the `deep-physics` binary (`src/main.rs`, stdin and
  stdout) and the wasm32 build (its `wasm` module: a module with no imports, one call line in, one reply line out),
  which `scripts/physics-wasm32.mjs` builds and runs (issue #2012).
- Rapier's own determinism tests (`snapshot_portability`, `simd_backend_determinism`, `simd_backend_parity`,
  `snapshot_roundtrip`) are this crate's `tests/`, upstream's files unchanged below a license header, so they build
  against this crate's pinned rapier3d (issue #2012). Their `#![cfg(feature = …)]` reads this crate's default
  `serde-serialize` and `enhanced-determinism` features, which forward to rapier3d's; never edit their bodies.
- A seek or physics.determinism goes in `src/runner/timeline.rs`; a view's frames in `src/runner/live.rs`; a wrong restore a criterion plants in `src/planted.rs`.
- The acceptance tests are in `tests/acceptance/2009`, `2010`, `2011`, `2012`, `1918`, `522`, `523`, `524`, `2457`, `191`, `2310` and `1931`; `scripts/physics-build.mjs` refuses a build with a feature or flag that changes results; `scripts/physics-budget.mjs` times release builds of this crate on each host.

## What it may import

`deep-canvas-physics` for the world, the caps, the interface, the bake key, the golden and the occurrence list; `deep-canvas-motion` to evaluate the paths a world reads of its scene; `deep-canvas-scene3d` for a soft volume's source mesh, a built item's or a mesh asset's glTF node's (#2457), and a balloon's ball (#524), in native builds only (the wasm32 build refuses balloons and volumes, naming #2466); scene3d links no C++, so the runner builds with a C compiler alone (#2738); `deep-canvas` for the SHA-256 a store file is checked against (#2457); `rapier3d` at `=0.36.1`; `bincode` for checkpoint bytes; `serde_json` for the runner; `simba` (`=0.10.2`, a dev-dependency) for Rapier's own `simd_backend_parity`. Outside the canvas lint: no Rapier type leaves this crate through the interface, and the build script and the guard's register reads, which the lint forbids, live here.

## Deviations

- `src/lib.rs` keeps Rapier's own contact prediction distance, scaled by `solver.length_unit` with its other lengths (#2308), and sleep's angular threshold, which no world field sets.
- `src/belt.rs` finds a pair's contacts anew at every step only where a kinematic body or a dynamic body a hinge's motor turns is in it (#2317). A dynamic body that turns with no motor on a hinge, one spun by a velocity or swung by its weight, still has its pairs' points kept, so what rests on it can slide; and things under 2 cm stacked on a turning body fall, since Rapier's stacks lean on the kept points. A body thinner than `solver.length_unit` ÷ 20 counts its own move in the world, not its move against what it rests on, so a stack of them carried along by a moving body finds its contacts anew at every step and leans on none; a world that sets `length_unit` to their size, as `physics.small_body` says, has no such body.
- `src/dim2.rs` sweeps no body (rapier2d's caps claim no `ccd`), writes no sensor rows and answers no query, character or vehicle call: the check refuses each in a 2D world. The physics module's manifest still names `build()`'s pins alone, so a 3D golden's digest is what it was; a 2D world's key and digest name `build_2d()`'s.
- `build.rs` runs `cargo tree --locked` for the build's own resolution: the lockfile is never changed, but cargo may fetch a git source the outer build never needed (issue #2012: with `--frozen`, a build in fresh linux containers failed on gpui-shell's quickjs-jit), so an offline build without those sources can't read its pins and fails.
- `src/main.rs` keeps bakes in a store folder by key as JSON, not yet in the evidence store, which no criterion of #2010 reads.
- `src/runner/live.rs` reads its threads' CPU time through `clock_gettime`, declared there, as the crate takes no `libc`. A view's worker is a thread of the `deep-physics` process, so its frames' CPU stands for the render worker's; the wasm32 build has no views.
- `src/runner/live.rs`'s live view keeps no rollback-window checkpoints of its own: no net module calls it yet (#551).
- `src/occurrences.rs` writes contact_begin, contact_end, hit, sensor_enter, sensor_exit, break, rest and wake, and `src/joints.rs` and `src/kinematic.rs` fault (speed_cap and jam) and crossing, and `src/soft.rs` writes `tear` and the soft bodies' `fault {kind: nan | capacity}`; no world here declares what writes `force` rows.
- `src/soft.rs` holds a closed body's volume constraint at 8 times its stretch's frequency, so its edges can't hold it below `inflate` times its rest volume, and lets a declared tear split off a piece of any size. Its contacts with rigid bodies write no occurrence rows.
- `src/lib.rs` continues a world with soft bodies from its own checkpoint at every checkpoint's step: Rapier's serialized world leaves out caches its soft contacts keep, so without it a seek and a run diverge at the first contact after the checkpoint.
- `src/joints.rs` counts a body's speed cap when a step starts past it or ends at it having started below it, since Rapier caps inside its solver and reports nothing; a body held at the cap writes one fault, not one a step, and so does a jam (`src/kinematic.rs`), written on the step it starts.
- `src/kinematic.rs` writes `fault {kind: jam}` on the step its solve first ends past its tolerance, naming the joint with the largest residual, and while jammed holds the nearest placement it can reach; it reports no residuals or branch flips to an audit, and a slider can't close a loop.
- `src/source.rs` builds a volume from a mesh asset only where a host hands the asset's bytes over: `deep-physics` with `evidence`, deep-show's bakes and deep-gpu's `view3d` (#2457). `src/cast.rs`'s caster is handed a scene with none, so `physics.cast@1` answers no hit on a world that holds such a volume.
- `src/articulated.rs` keeps Rapier's default damping on an articulation's turning coordinates (0.1 N·m·s/rad), which no world field sets and `caps()` declares: with none, a light chain let go level blows up. What of two authored poses' turn a joint's own freedom can't hold goes into its second frame, so the body starts as written. The engine overwrites the velocity of a body of an articulation from its coordinates at every step, so a velocity written on one is refused at check, a launch, a poke and a release that stills one are refused at their step (`src/inputs.rs`), and a grab's force moves it.
- `src/scene.rs`'s cycle check follows drivers' vars and lists back to the world's outputs and forms; a follow channel or a parent that reads a physics pose isn't traced.
