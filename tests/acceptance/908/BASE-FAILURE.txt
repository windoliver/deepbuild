== A sample fix, kept outside this checkout (a scratch copy of a7e140b2; not committed anywhere)

What it does, about 70 lines: setup drafts asks.notice (1h per kind); a new core event ask.moved@1 (subject the ask,
data span, deadline, because away|notice), projected into the asks table's deadline; on each settle the core takes
the gap since its last beat (or, after a start, since the last hub.stopped) when it is over 2 s, and moves each open
ask that hadn't shown by the part of the gap before its deadline as it stood; and once an ask has shown, a deadline
less than its kind's notice after its first showing moves to that notice.
