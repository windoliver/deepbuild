## The planted program (throwaway, in a scratch folder, deleted, never committed)

To check that the surface can be met and that each twin fails, I laid a planted cache into scratch copies of this
checkout. The copies held `scripts/`, this folder and a link to `node_modules`. The cache was about 70 lines in
`bays.mjs`: a `Pair.build(role, early, run)` that `inBay` calls in place of `build(bay)`. Its key is a SHA-256 of the
tree recipe (tree, bundle and untracked hashes), the overlay hash when the overlay is laid before the build,
`node --version` and `npm --version` from PATH, and the bay's lock hash. A stored entry is
`<HOME>/deepbuild-bays/cache/<key>/` holding the files the build made (`files/<path>`) and `meta.json` (their SHA-256s
and the time stored). On a hit, every stored file is checked against its hash before it is copied into the bay. It
prints `BUILD {"role","use","tree","key","reused"[,"why"]}`. It evicts the oldest entries until the cache is under
`DEEP_BUILD_CACHE_BYTES`, before each read and after each store.
