## Known limits

A word can sit in what was not taken rather than in the decision; the five `Not taken` are counted, not matched to
decisions; a surface named as not plain reads as named; an anchor the base didn't have counts wherever in the section
it sits; an issue is told by words; and who wrote the record isn't something a file tells. The taste call covers the
first three; the README lists the rest under Not asserted.
