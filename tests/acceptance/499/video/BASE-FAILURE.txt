The suite was not built or run at the base: the job was told not to run cargo or vitest, since the machine's disk was
nearly full. What follows is read from the base's code, not from its output.

The surfaces these tests read (README: The surface under test) don't exist at the base:

- deep-show run
  - No image@1 plays a video: crates/deep-canvas-marks/src/scene.rs reads image@1's `asset` as a still picture, with
    no `frames`, `anchors` or video probe; nothing in the hub sends an image's asset to the decode worker.
  - `eval` has no `video` block (crates/deep-show/src/main.rs: `eval` takes debug_text, doc, t, view, clean,
    perturb, pixels, film, size, arrays), and `determinism`'s `play` and `seek` sides carry only `pixels` and
    `nodes` (crates/deep-show/src/time.rs).
  - The scenario's `planted` knows #498's names and others, not `lookup_floors_frame_rate` or `seek_reads_proxy`, so a
    run with either answers {"ok": false, "code": "bad_input"}.
- deep-canvas-render
  - `render` draws image@1 from `store` as a still picture (crates/deep-marks-render/src/asset.rs); an MP4 isn't one,
    and there is no NV12 path or shader taking the probe's matrix and range. `planted` knows `gpui_oklab_gradient`
    only, so `stock_bt601_fragment` answers bad_input.
  - `idle` takes no `store` and no `pans`, and its answer has no `players`, no `views` and no `allocs` on `drawn`.

So, expected at the base (not observed):

- frames.test.ts (2), interval.test.ts (1), proxy.test.ts (1): each fails at its first read of `video`
