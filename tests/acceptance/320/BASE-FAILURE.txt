== The sample fix

A sample fix, kept in a scratch copy of the base outside this checkout (not committed, not part of this change), to
show the tests can pass. 27 lines added to packages/asks/src/index.ts: an ask whose owner isn't the connection's actor
is marked as someone else's, left out of inbox() and never shown; when it's an unblock, the core appends a drafted
request right after it, an authority ask (send / don't send) from a new ask.sources@1 row `request.draft`, with the
block's handle as its event's subject and the block's pinned_to as its own, so #318's card `changed` re-checks it
against the worktree each time its card is made; inbox rows carry `request`; cards carry `cost_usd`. In the schemas,
ask.created@1 and @2's unblock branch get minItems 1 on steps and unblocks and an optional cost_usd (edited in place,
digests not updated: the sample only has to show the tests can pass).
