== The sample fix

A sample fix, kept in a scratch copy of the base outside this checkout (not committed, not part of this change), to
show the tests can pass: about 190 lines added to packages/asks/src/index.ts on #318's core. It reads <home>/config.json
and the flow-map lines of a repo's done.yaml asks: block, merged key by key; fills the deadline and default at raise;
records each show as an ask.batched event (a batch "batch:<ms>" or "now:<ms>"); runs batches with the day's cap; shows
at once an ask held by holds(), or one whose deadline (the exception's: the earliest it holds) comes before the next
batch; holds an ask raised after a person's unread message until the session's next tool.before; and adds Later,
inbox() and the exception's "together" bulk answer. No schema changed.
