## Not run, and known limits

- Catalogue drift ran nowhere: neither this checkout nor the scratch trees hold `packages/contracts/dist`, and this
  job starts no build. Its findings reader was held only against lines in the format `scripts/catalogue-drift.mjs`
  prints. So the doc lanes test has not been seen to pass, only to fail and to skip.
- No live test has been seen to pass on GitHub: nothing of the work is there. Their pass paths ran against the
  stand-in only.
- The base is not on `origin/main`. Every test file but `ac4-told.live.test.mjs` reads it with git by its full hash,
  so a checkout that doesn't hold that commit fails them, naming it.
- A decision's words can sit in what was not taken, or in a line about something else; an audit line can hold a
  thing's words in a new sentence and say nothing of it; a link counts from any line that holds a decision's words,
  whatever the issue says there; and a comment can hold the ID and the word and tell no one. Criterion 4's reader
  covers those.
