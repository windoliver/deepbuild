   before any frame draws. It has no `leader` or `hide`, and its `overlays` rows have no `leader`. Both fixture guards
   ran and held first (every sampled projection in front of the camera and at least 0.5 px from the safe frame's
   edge; 136 inside, 64 outside; the label's frame 30 px clear).

   logic-array lines, so the wire's are missing. Read from the code: its `lid#hinge` point is the lid's origin
   (`point_of`: "an item's evaluated anchor (its origin)"), which projects up to 57 px from lid#hinge at the sampled
   times inside the safe frame (the test's guard), so with its segments reported it would still fail.
