scripts/proof-runs.mjs). These ten read no spike, so no work could make them fail there. The ID was taken out of those
ten names, which now start `AC8 rejects`, `AC9 rejects` and `AC12 rejects`; no assertion, no validator and no other

their tests in record.test.mjs, which fail at the base as listed.
