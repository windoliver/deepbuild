### mac-only-dependency (crates/deep-app/src/show.rs), AC1 #1121.73d8f179

`objc2::runtime::Bool`, of a crate deep-app's macOS dependencies hold and its other sources use.

## A program that passes, not kept

To see that AC3's tests can pass, the blind job wrote a throwaway change to `runLanes` of about 40 lines: on the Mac
`list` from `cargo test … -- --list`, and on Linux the rows, the state and the summary line from the Mac's record.
With it the Mac's test passed on the Mac and the two Spark tests passed on the Spark (the third skipped on each). It
is not in this folder and was shown to no one.
