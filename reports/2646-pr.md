## Counter Failure Isolated

A follow-up diagnostic runs the same small Node child through the unchanged
`measureOnce`, without any benchmark fixture or hub history:

| Launch shape | Program exit | Instructions | Uncounted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary Node child | 0 | 1,053,958,459 | none |
| Supervisor helper's `leash` | 0 | missing | `(forked)` |
| Protected `/usr/bin/sandbox-exec`, then a Node child | 0 | missing | `a child of /usr/bin/sandbox-exec` |

Thus the counter exists, the programs succeed, and both missing-count shapes
reproduce independently of #2646's history optimizations.
`native/helper.c` forks a leash watcher that terminates with `_exit`.
The counter records un-reaped children through a normal-exit destructor,
but does not interpose `_exit`. The protected-executable path additionally
loses the injected library; the counter explicitly documents that boundary.
Neither is repaired by changing session reads or benchmark fixture versions.

The two-minute test-grant request was refused. This small diagnostic then ran
through `scripts/lease`, at nice 10, without a grant; it is explicitly not a
paired budget run or performance verdict. Actual output is appended to
`2646-pr-evidence.txt`. No failing budget was retried or reclassified.

A repair must extend the approved measurement tool to cover these real child
processes, not remove the watcher, bypass Seatbelt, exclude children, or replace
missing counts. That requires an approved check proposal; this PR does not
self-approve one. The diagnosis did not prove or waive criterion 5; the owner
subsequently approved deferring that requirement as recorded below.
