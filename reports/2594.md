## Requirement → change → proof

| Criterion | Change | Results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AC1: concurrent core folders get their requested mode | Remove the temporary process-wide `0177` umask around listen; keep startup's `0077` mask | Frozen `startup.test.ts` fails at the real worker's `agents/syncs` EACCES on the base, then passes unchanged on macOS and Linux: both directories are `0700` during bind, hub ready, owner receives hello |
| AC2: another user cannot open the socket from creation | Secure new **and existing** hub roots to `0700` before starting the worker/socket; retain final socket `0600` | Both frozen tests pass on macOS and Linux. `existing-root.test.ts` fails on the macOS base at the creation-boundary privacy assertion; no private test ancestor is counted |
| Existing transport behavior | Same socket path, lock/lifecycle, listener and final mode | All three unchanged #16 socket tests pass on macOS: installed/dev ownership and privacy across hooks/restart, plus no TCP/UDP listener |

The fix belongs in the existing transport startup: an extension cannot secure a socket before that transport exists. No new option, dependency, export, production test hook or repair of old damaged folders is introduced.
