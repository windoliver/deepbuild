## Why the core changes are necessary

The extension owns discovery, copying and asks. Evidence integrity/retention and `session:` resolution are core-owned, so a private extension folder cannot provide the existing public handle semantics. The evidence handle remains the SHA-256 of actual concatenated text, not a manifest hash. New manifests retain ordinary immutable evidence parts, verified on read and kept by retention. Persisted SHA-256 continuation state avoids decompressing old evidence after restart; it is checked against the stored digest, and block/Unicode boundaries are compared with Node SHA-256.

The production/schema work exceeds 400 handwritten lines because archive writes, evidence storage/retention, event catalogue and record readers must agree on one compatible representation. Splitting them into independent landings would expose unreadable archive links. The much larger test addition is the immutable independent job plus its already-run blind redrafts; none was discarded because it fails.
