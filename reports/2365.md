No oracle, fixture, twin, expected value or test name changed (`repair/repair.diff`, 70 lines).
- **A match that starts in copied lines and ends in added ones (`c69d0d419`, `98aea60d9`).** A pass over a grown transcript appended the scrub of the new text alone. Known multi-line openings were held back, but not every rule: an LDIF value folded onto lines that arrived after a pass was stored unmasked. The worker now reads the copied prefix's last whole lines again, within the scrub's reach, and scrubs them with what follows. If a mask crosses the old end, or those lines scrub differently alone, nothing is appended and the copy starts again from the first byte. The check knows no rule.
- **A copy that never ended (same commits).** `scrubPrefix` asked `lastIndexOf` for offset -1 when a mask began at the text's first byte. That searches from the end, so the cut never moved and the worker spun. A two-line held value that was all of an unterminated transcript reproduced it. Copies of one worker queue behind each other, so one such transcript would have stopped every later copy.
- **Proof for both.** `packages/archive/test/growth-context.test.ts`: one test per defect, and a seeded property (40 cases, seed 2365) that writes generated transcripts in arbitrary steps, passes after each, and holds every copy to the whole scrub of the bytes its event says it read. On `c15e891ea` the first test fails on the unmasked lines and the second times out (`context/before.txt`); the property run hung on the same loop and was stopped by its PID. On `98aea60d9` all three pass (`context/after.txt`).
- **Lint (`7cc5409c5`).** Two fixtures spelled a made-up private-key armor that the secrets lint read as a key. They are built from parts now. Two saved outputs under `reports/2365/memory/` quoted the same armor and say `(synthetic)` now. `repo-rules.mjs` reports nothing in a file this PR changes.

3. **Memory bound.** A line, or the lines one mask covers, is one unit and is never split, as the Scope requires. ## Why the core changes, and the size

The extension owns discovery, copying and asks. Evidence integrity, retention and `session:` resolution are the core's, so a private archive folder could not keep the public handles. The evidence handle stays the SHA-256 of the concatenated text. The production and schema work is over 400 handwritten lines because archive writes, evidence storage, the event catalogue and the record's readers must agree on one representation; landing them apart would expose unreadable links. Most of the diff is the independent acceptance suite, its two redrafts and generated contract output.
