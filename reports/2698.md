## Needs your decision

1. **The perf lane should read FAIL for `hub-idle` against its merge base. It was not run, so this is read from `scripts/benchmark.mjs`'s `compare`.** AC5's 15 s to settle takes the task from about 8 s to about 21 s, past the 50% margin on p50. `deep-status-30` and `deep-mcp-30d` now do more work too, so their instruction comparison will likely read above 2%. No budget was touched.
2. **What to do about AC5.** I did not find what the first run's extra work was, and I changed nothing to chase it. What is known:
   - Its 5 s held about 154 M instructions where a settled 5 s holds about 35 M, with wakeups nearly the same (200 against 175).
   - Two hubs read once a second for 75 s afterwards, at loads of 247 and 299 ([first](2698/idle-trace-first.txt), [second](2698/idle-trace-second.txt); a diagnostic, not a test), were steady at 5 to 9 M a second from second 13 on. Seconds 16 to 20 averaged 7.08 and 7.02 M. Both had one second of 46 M at second 47.
   - Before that the hub is still starting: about 100 wakeups a second for nine seconds, then 140 to 190 M instructions a second for three. `hub-idle` counts its 15 s from the socket's answer, and that phase ended 3.5 s before the window in both traces. At a load near 600 the window read 84 M a second.
   - The Nov 13 audit's own outlier, 10.4 M a second over 30 s against 6.3 to 7.1, is about the same excess, 110 M.

   So one burst of about 110 M inside the span breaks 10%, at 5 s or at 30 s. The choices I see: dispute AC5 to a blind redraft, as the design has it; give `hub-idle` several rounds and compare medians; or leave it open until #2648 removes the idle work. AC5's test was held for a 1-minute load under 200, a rule written into the run script before any task ran.

## Choices the issue left open

- **Wakeups** are the kernel's interrupt wakeups. Package idle wakeups are a subset of them and read 0 here.
- **Children** are direct children. A grandchild is not read.
- **A child that ended inside the span** is left out whole: its work before it ended is in no sum.
- **`deep-status-30`'s counted run is a second `deep status`** in the same round, not the timed one, so `statusMs` is measured as before.
- **`deep-mcp-30d`'s rounds** are the one round it times. Its instructions are `deep mcp`'s over that round, not from its start.
- **`hub-idle`'s span** stays the 5 s it already measured over. #2676 plans 60 s.
- **`hub-cold-start`** reads the hub from its start to just after the socket answers. The hub works on between the answer and the reading, a few ms.
- **The reader's binary** is built once a machine into `~/Library/Caches/deepbuild-perf/`, beside the perf lane's counter, when a benchmark loads.
- **`scripts/lanes.mjs`** lists `tests/acceptance/2698/` as alone, as #1405's folder is: each file reads live processes under load.
