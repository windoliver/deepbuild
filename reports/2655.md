## Where this differs from the issue

- **AC1's merge-base sentence no longer holds as written.** The issue was read at `ccbdefd1f`. #2332 (`c332f7fb0`) landed after it and moved the adapter registry out of `hookmap.ts`, so today's base loads no evidence module on the main thread. It still loaded the validator, through the adapter registry's own registration check and through `guards.ts`. The test fails on the base for those seven contracts modules, not for "both entries".
- **Two public entries were added**: `@deepbuild/contracts/json` and `@deepbuild/adapters/unchecked`. AC1 and AC3's 0.5 MB can't be met without them: TLS alone read 0.26 MB. I asked which way to go and was told to take the one I recommend; this is it.
- **The event loop no longer checks adapter facts.** The parser's registry holds the same files to the published shapes when it starts (`warm`), and the core's does too. With facts that fail the check the parser fails as before, and no line is parsed.
- **`process.getBuiltinModule('node:tls')`, not `await import`.** An await between the "is it open" test and the assignment would let two calls both open a listener.
- **The key is rebuilt from its bytes.** In V8 a slice, and what a global replace pieces together, keep the whole command alive: 2,000 keys read 446 MB with a plain slice and 15.97 MB with the copy. A lone surrogate in a command becomes U+FFFD in its key, on the live path and the restored one alike.

## Found on the way, not changed

- **A short hook line still crosses with its whole pool.** Node 24.22 takes buffers under 32 KiB from a 64 KiB pool, and a post with no transfer list copies the whole pool: a 100-byte line arrives as a view of a 65,536-byte buffer. The base does the same. The scope hands over a line only "where the line is its own buffer", so this is left.
