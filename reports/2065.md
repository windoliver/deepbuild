## Gaps closed during PR preparation

Each started with a saved behavioral failure, then passed unchanged:
- Typed words `accept`, `split` or `fund` do not select those options. `ask.answered@6` records the chosen option separately; replay shares its interpretation with count resets.
- Overlapping stops advance distinct rounds instead of recording [1,1].
- A funded earlier episode cannot hide a later interrupted stall's missing ask.
- A crash after durable progress but before its send-back receipt recovers that receipt once.
- Expired incomplete budget approval cannot prevent startup; it extends no authority.
- Concurrent linked worktrees raise one switch ask; large open-item lists use a bounded equality digest; agents cannot attach done-gate actions.

The regression files are under `packages/{hub,budget,gate,record}/test/`. Fail-before evidence for the final review findings and pass-after output are in `reports/2065/`. `gate-record.ts` contains the record-only operations; `stop-judging.ts` remains at 1,500 lines without raising a ceiling. No existing test, golden, contract shape, known-failure entry or check was changed to pass.

## Scope and size

An extension cannot recover associations the existing done gate never recorded. This change uses its current asks/record/budget boundaries; no dependency, setting or public package export was added. The patch exceeds 400 lines because four versioned event shapes, their generated TS/Rust, the independent acceptance suite and targeted regression proof must ship with the persistence behavior. Most line churn is generated or schema data; splitting it would leave a required action or its proof incomplete.

Pre-upgrade asks without structured action metadata are not retroactively migrated: the old record lacks the required associations, and prose is not trusted as a substitute. Pre-existing default/undo behavior (a default split's drafted items and the budget ledger's `default.undone`) is not changed. These are outside the six tested criteria; no criterion of #2065 is left open.
