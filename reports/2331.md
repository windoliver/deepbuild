## The kit

Written blind at `1c4d1f0e` from the issue, the design and the base, before the fix. The hashes of the seven drafted
files are in [acceptance.sha256](2331/acceptance.sha256). Two of them differ now, by edits that change no assertion,
fixture, timeout or expected output:

- `kit.ts` has one more line, a comment that writes the two criterion IDs out. Its README asks for that before
  freezing: the job had no shell to compute them, and the proof lane finds a test by a literal tag.
- `tsconfig.json` leaves out `twins/`, whose files are built where they are laid, not as suite modules.

`twins/locale-clock` and `twins/utc-clock` each hold `packages/hub/src/list.ts` whole, because the proof lane lays
whole files over the change. The first puts the locale formatters back; the second writes the clock in UTC.
`twins.json` names the first for criterion 1 and the second for criterion 2.

The app was not changed. The issue's `notify.rs:clock_time` is stale at this base: notifications call
`states::moment`, which formats local time with chrono and keeps the app's words.
