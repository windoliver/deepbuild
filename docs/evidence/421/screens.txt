#421 screenshots: Mac15,7, Metal, Nov 3

There are two sheets, each drawn from the same six scene documents:

- window-e2e.png:
  - What: each scene sent as an agent's `deep_show` and drawn in deep-show's native window (`deep-show screen`), with the session's default look.
  - Results: all six were `presented` with no issues, and the window process loaded no web engine (checked from dyld's library list).
- view3d-metal.png: `deep-gpu run` view3d final targets, filmic tone map at exposure 0.

The panels:

1. Area lights: a 2700 K rect on the left and a 9000 K disk on the right, at 40 lm each, with no sun.
2. Coat and multiple scattering, from left to right:
   - rough red;
   - the same red with a clear coat (its sharp highlight);
   - rough gold, which keeps its brightness through multiple scattering;
   - polished gold, which is black outside its highlight because there's no environment to reflect yet (#422).
3. See-through:
   - three blend panes composited back to front;
   - an additive glow (left);
   - a mask pane at alpha 0.7, drawn opaque (right).
4. Shadow catcher: on a catcher over an unlit backdrop, only the boxes' shadows draw.
5. The ten procedural patterns in each block's own space. Faces turned from the sun are black because these scenes have no sky ambient.
6. 48 point lights at 12 lm each: 32 are shaded, and the frame's `lights_over_budget` notice names the 16 left out.

Scene 6 found a bug, fixed in this PR. The light policy scored a light 0 whenever the eye was outside the light's range. So a 12 lm light, with a 4.4 m auto range and 6 m from the eye, went unshaded, and its floor rendered black.
- The fix: the policy now drops a light only when its range misses the frustum.
- The regression test is `light::tests::a_light_whose_range_reaches_the_view_but_not_the_eye_is_shaded`, which fails on the old rule.
