When an ask shows and what happens if nobody answers: batches, deadlines drafted at setup, fallbacks and announced defaults, and the inbox projection the CLI and the app read.

**Epic:** #2 · **Part of:** #22 · **Design:** [An ask, and your answer getting back](https://github.com/windoliver/deepbuild/blob/main/docs/technical-design.md#an-ask-and-your-answer-getting-back) · [Decided](https://github.com/windoliver/deepbuild/blob/main/docs/technical-design.md#decided)

### Scope
- Batching with a daily cap; an ask shows at once when a session is held waiting on it, or when its deadline would pass before the next batch, cap or not, so no ask reaches its deadline unseen
- Inbox projection with status, owner and age; past its deadline an unanswered ask goes to its fallback owner, then the queue you named, then its announced, undoable default; none expires silently
- Once one session holds five open asks (a value drafted at setup), its further asks fold into one exception, "this session keeps asking", answered in bulk, used to pause the session, or left to the defaults

### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Asks beyond the cap wait for the next batch unless a session is held or the ask's deadline would pass first
- [ ] With the hub stopped across a batch time, each open ask whose deadline comes before the next batch shows in Needs you within 5 s of the hub's start; a hub that waits for the next batch fails this test, naming the ask
- [ ] For one person, an authority ask past its 1-day deadline takes no at that deadline, recorded as defaulted, never as escalated; a hub that records it as escalated with nobody to take it fails this test, naming the ask
- [ ] At its deadline an ask takes its default, announced and undoable; a default never makes a claim pass, anything irreversible defaults to no, and Later never passes the deadline
- [ ] A sixth open ask from one session lands inside one "this session keeps asking" exception, and answering it in bulk closes all six
- [ ] With batches 8 hours apart and the day's cap already reached, an early question with its 4-hour deadline raised just after a batch shows at once, a taste call with its 3-day deadline waits for the next batch, and no ask takes its default before it has shown
- [ ] Setup drafts each kind's deadline (taste 3 days, authority 1 day, unblock, exception and choice 2 days); at its deadline an authority ask takes no, a taste call or a choice takes its recommended option, and an early question locks the definition without its requirement, so the PR can only say "Part of"
- [ ] With five taste asks open in one session, a sixth, an authority ask with a 1-day deadline, folds into the exception, and the exception shows at once when that deadline would pass before the next batch; at its deadline that ask alone takes no, and an ask held for a message the agent hasn't read shows the same way
- [ ] In a repo whose done.yaml sets deadlines only for taste and authority, a choice ask takes the choice deadline and default from your own settings, and never fails its schema or reads as undeclared
- [ ] Budgets, on the reference Mac in the local preflight, compared with the merge base: creating or answering an ask takes under 5 ms in the hub; the inbox projection with 10,000 asks loads in under 100 ms
- [ ] No ask is ever closed silently: past its deadline it goes to its fallback, then its announced default, and each step is recorded
