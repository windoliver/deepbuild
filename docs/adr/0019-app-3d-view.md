# 0019: What the app's 3D view draws, and what it is handed

Status: accepted (#3517). #2494's decision stands: the app's canvas panel draws a 3D writer layer through a GPU-layer view, and a revision the view can't draw shows its reason, never a frame from another path. The issue's Scope says what the view must come to draw, and the suite's README, `tests/acceptance/3517/README.md`, gives the fixture's keys and the planted programs. The decisions below are the ones written nowhere else.

## What the hub hands the app for a 3D layer

- **`scene` names the form the frames draw from.** `crates/deep-show/src/hub/presented.rs`, `presented_revision`. The canvas core expands each pack recipe's instance into its derived items, as render-back does before it evaluates (`Hub::expanded3d`), and keeps that form in the evidence store by its hash. The app holds no pack store, so it evaluates what the core resolved. A document with no instance is handed as its canonical document, as before.
- **A 3D layer's 2D items come as its picture.** `crates/deep-show/src/hub/presented.rs`, `presented_revision`. The first layer of a 3D document carries the whole document's 2D items, rastered with no ground at t = 0, in the look the document names, as `Hub::items2d` prepares them for render-back, at twice the panel's size in points: the px of the densest display a Mac has, where the slot under it is drawn. At the panel's size in points a glyph snaps to another grid, and the text sat 0.8 px from render-back's. The panel paints it over the slot (`crates/deep-app/src/show.rs`, `CanvasPanel::render`). It is a still, as every 2D layer's picture in the app is. A layer that shows a reason paints no picture, so a document is never drawn in part.
- **A reply to your input carries the session's state.** `crates/deep-canvas/src/scope.rs`, `Scope::to_json`. The scope's `camera` gains `user`: your offsets on the camera (`azimuth`, `elevation`, `zoom`, `pan`), beside the pose they give. The app applies the offsets, never the pose, since the pose in the reply is the scope's at its own time and the view's camera moves with t.

## How the view draws by the session

- **The view's time is the playhead's while the transport is paused, else its own clock's.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `SceneView::steer` and `SceneView::t`. The canvas core in the hub plays its calls on a virtual clock, so it has no time to hand a playing view: the view counts from when it was made. A playhead holds the view at that time and draws one frame. When the playhead clears, the clock runs on from it. The scope has no rate control, so the view scales nothing.
- **A paused view asks for a frame only while the frame it drew last is of another time.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `SceneView::paint`. A frame skipped for want of a free slot is asked for again, and once the frame at the playhead is on screen the window is asked for nothing.
- **Your offsets turn the camera after the scene is evaluated.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `evaluated`. `deep_canvas_scene3d::frames::turned_camera` gives the camera at t under the offsets, as the proof binary's live view turns it. Where a shot poses the camera, the program is compiled from the posed document, so the offsets ride the shot.
- **A shot's pose is written before the scene is evaluated, and such a frame is due again as soon as it shows.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `evaluated` and `render`. `deep_canvas_motion::shots::posed` is the pose render-back draws through.

## What the view holds and what it still refuses

- **The view holds the canvas modules it seals.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `held`. Text, look, marks2d, motion, scene3d, effects and building, sealed once a process; an evaluation reads the seal's outline sources and module fills, as render-back's reads its build's. A document that requires any other module is refused `module_missing`, naming it.
- **A look is read as render-back reads it.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `dressed`. The look a document names is read from the evidence store by the hash its `assets` give, its roles resolved over the bundled faces, with the status distance held only where the document binds status. One that can't be read is the layer's reason at `look/asset`.
- **Refused, each named in the layer's place.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `HOSTED`, `dressed` and `evaluated`. `arrange@1`, whose places the hub's session solves; `overlay.view@1`, a second frame of the document; `overlay.frame@1`, whose 2D frame moves with a projected point each frame; a look's operator finish (`look/finish`), which runs over a frame's bytes; a drawing look (`look/drawn`), whose passes live in `deep-groups-render`, a crate the app can't take: it reaches the app again through `deep-marks-render` and `deep-replica`; a lens dirt texture (`look/bloom/dirt`); a material's maps and an inset's picture, which render-back's decode worker and 2D raster make.
- **A reason is recorded in English and shown in the app's language.** `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `Reason`. The record and the count read `recorded`; the panel paints `shown`.

## The record and the count

- **The app reports a refused layer with `canvas.drawn`.** `crates/deep-app/src/show.rs`, `CanvasPanel::refused` and `follow`; `packages/hub/src/hub-canvas.ts`, `recordRefused`. Each `{doc, rev, layer, reason}` is sent until the hub has taken it, and the hub appends one `layer.refused` for each it hasn't recorded since it started.
- **The count asks the view's own code, with no window, no GPU and no hub.** `crates/deep-app/src/canvas_fixture.rs`, `count`; `crates/deep-app/src/scene_view.rs`, `undrawn`. Every `.json` file under `packs/examples/` and `tests/acceptance/` that holds a 3D item is loaded and evaluated once at t = 0 at its own size. An asset a document names by hash is read from the `.json` files beside it: those under `packs/examples/`, or under its own suite's folder, by their SHA-256, hashed the first time a document of that folder asks. So a look a fixture keeps as a file is read, and a mesh, an image or an HDR is not: the count reads no binary file, and a copy of the tree's JSON files counts the same. A document whose asset reads as missing is counted under the reason the view gives for it. A `node_modules` folder is never read.
- **The kept count only falls.** `scripts/view-count.mjs`. The file's refused documents may not outnumber the merge base's with `origin/main`, by hand or by `--write`. Until the merge base holds a count there is nothing to compare with.
