# 0011: The circuit@2 pack, its reference library and the script that writes it

Status: accepted (#3715). The design states circuit@2 under Logic: library interfaces (`docs/canvas-primitives.md`, As built #3604) and the pack's `LAYOUT.md` states its layouts, its solve and its planted libraries; the decisions below are the ones written nowhere else.

## The library

- **The library computes its numbers by plain IEEE operations only.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `ab`, `sqrt`, `sincos_turns`, `exp_neg`. Every host then gives the same bits. `sqrt` takes Newton's steps from the exponent's guess; its last two steps agree or are neighbours, and it keeps the smaller. `sincos_turns` takes turns, reduces them exactly to the nearest quarter turn and runs the kernels on ±π/4, whose coefficients are the classic minimax ones, good to an ulp or so. `exp_neg` is e^x for x ≤ 0: x = k ln 2 + r with |r| ≤ ln 2 ÷ 2, the classic rational kernel for e^r, and a subnormal result scaled in two steps.
- **The library keeps everything in the caller's memory and calls nothing.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `at`; `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `library`. It is rustc's relocatable object of functions only, importing nothing but the caller's memory, and every address is an i32 into that memory (#3604).
- **Each source frequency is one solve of the same matrix.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `network`, `regroup`. Every closed part is linear, so a frequency's sources go in as phasors of their rms values, DC as frequency 0, and each instant is the closed form at the step's time, f × k ÷ per turns. The groups are DC first when any DC source is added, then each AC source's frequency as it stands, in part order.
- **A column with no pivot left sets its unknown to 0 and flags the result singular.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `eliminate`. It is Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting, in place, on `d` unknowns and `m` right-hand sides.
- **A branch's current runs from its first node to its second.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `network`. A branch current j from `fr` to `t` leaves `fr` and enters `t`, and its row is V(t) − V(fr) = e. A source behind its internal resistance is stamped as e ÷ r into `to` and out of `from`.
- **The closed network's parts are rooted by a union-find kept in the state, the lower root winning.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `find`, `UP`. Node 0's part is then rooted at 0, and a node whose root is itself and not 0 is the lowest node of a part nothing joins to node 0. A node no closed part touches is such a part on its own.

## The script

- **A run writes `objects/`, `fixtures/`, `src/*.wat` and `pack.json` whole, and keeps `src/circuit.rs` and `LAYOUT.md`.** `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `OUT`. circuit@1's pack is `scripts/circuit-pack.mjs`'s, and nothing in this script changes it (#1181, #3604).
- **The fixtures' programs are WebAssembly text, assembled by an assembler the caller names.** `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `assembler`, `program`. It is run as `node scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs <assembler>`, and the assembler takes `<out-dir> --program <file.wat>`; `tests/acceptance/416/interfaces/wat` builds one.
- **A fixture's program has one memory map.** `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `READS_AT`, `WRITES_AT`, `DATA_AT`, `ASSETS_AT`, `STATE_AT`, `PAGES`. The read region is at 0, the writes at 4096, 8 bytes of data at 49152, the built table (asset 0) at `assets_at + 4` and the state at 1 MiB, in 32 pages. `init` adds every node, then every part with its kind's builder; each step writes the item's own arrays the circuit reads, solves the circuit's step k at the item's rate and copies the state's head over the write region.
- **The reference library's own fixture holds only values that are exact.** `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `ownFixture`. It is a lamp behind a breaker on the mains that imports the library by its own name, and it is compared exactly (#190's rule).
- **`plants` names seven planted libraries.** `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `PLANTED`, `manifest`. They are the issue's five wrong programs by name, and the kit's twins for `circuit.fuse` and `circuit.range` (#3604).

## What the names mean

- **`BASE` is the memory addresses are offsets from, off wasm.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `BASE`. A host's tests set it.
- **`DIM` is the most unknowns, and `E` the elementary charge in coulombs.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `DIM`, `E`. The unknowns are every node but the reference and two branches for every part.
- **The library's own bytes start at `o = s + OWN_AT` with ten scalars.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `READY`, `NN`, `PP`, `TABLE`, `VALID`, `NG`, `SINGULAR`, `K0`, `PER`, `LAST`. At +0 an i32 that is 1 once a builder ran, at +4 and +8 the i32 counts of nodes and parts, at +12 the i32 table address, at +16 an i32 saying the closed set's solve is current, at +20 the i32 count of groups, at +24 an i32 singular, at +32 the i64 step the closed set last changed, at +40 the i64 last per and at +48 the i64 last step solved. From +64 come the per-part arrays, each `PARTS` long, then the groups', then the solve's.
- **The per-part arrays of inputs and protective state.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`. `ADDED` is i32 × PARTS, 1 once added, and `NODE_ADDED` i32 × NODES. `LATEST` is f64 × PARTS × 3, each input's latest value. `RAW` is f64 × PARTS × 2, each set field's latest value as given, and `VAL` the same shape, each set field's value held to its range. `THETA` is f64 × PARTS, a fuse's or a breaker's heat. `OPEN` is i32 × PARTS: a fuse melted, a breaker or an RCD open. `MAG` is i32 × PARTS, a breaker's magnetic element reached. `COUNT` is f64 × PARTS, an RCD's count. `COOL` is f64 × PARTS, a fuse's or breaker's e^(−dt ÷ τ) at the last per. `X0` is f64 × PARTS, a wire's displacement at the step `K0`.
- **`CODE` is i32 × PARTS, the closed set solved.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `CODE`, `update`. A part's code is 0 out of the network; 1 closed (a changeover at `a`, an RCD's poles closed); 2 a changeover at `b`; 3 a changeover joining both, which only the planted `both_ways` gives. An RCD adds 2 while its test path conducts.
- **The per-part arrays of a solve.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`. `BRANCH` is i32 × PARTS × 2, a part's branch unknowns, or −1. `RMS` is f64 × PARTS, its rms current under the closed set, and `RMS2` its second pole's or winding's. `RESID` is f64 × PARTS, an RCD's residual, and `MEAN` f64 × PARTS, a part's mean power.
- **The groups' arrays and the solve's.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`. `FREQ` is f64 × GROUPS, each group's frequency, 0 for DC; `S0` and `C0` are f64 × GROUPS, sin and cos at `K0`. `IPH` is f64 × PARTS × GROUPS × 2, each part's current phasor (re, im), and `I2PH` the same for its second pole or winding. `VPH` is f64 × NODES × GROUPS × 2, each node's volts phasor. `MAT` is f64 × DIM × DIM and `RHS` f64 × 2 GROUPS × DIM. `UP` is i32 × NODES, a union-find's parents. `SC` is f64 × GROUPS × 2, each group's sin and cos at the step.
- **`start` starts the state from the table at `g` at the first builder.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `start`. It is false when another table started the state, or the table's counts are out of range.
- **The readers of a part.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `nd`, `set_mode`, `held`, `val`, `group_of`, `ends`, `branches`. `nd` is node j (0 to 3) of part i. `set_mode` is set field j (0 or 1) of part i: 0 a number, 1 a control, 2 an f64 the item writes. `held` is a value held to set field j's range; one that isn't finite keeps `last`. `val` is number j of part i as it stands: its set value when set, else the table's. `group_of` is the index of a part's frequency among the groups. `ends` is the nodes branch w (0 or 1) of part i joins at code c: a changeover's common and the way it is at, an RCD's and a transformer's pole or winding w, any other's `from` and `to`. `branches` is how many branches of its own part i takes at code c.
- **The readers of an input.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `latest`, `input_now`, `set_now`, `took`, `update`. `latest` is the latest value of the control whose slot is at `r + off`: its last entry this step, else what it was. `input_now` is input w of part i (0 what closes it, 1 `reset_by`, 2 `test_by`) at this solve: a control's latest value, or the i32 or bool the item wrote this step, and `was` with none. `set_now` is set field j of part i as given at this solve. `took` is input w of part i at this solve, kept as its latest, as (what it was, what it is). `update` takes part i's inputs, protective state and set values at this solve and gives its code and whether a set value changed.
- **The matrix's writers and the solve's results.** `packs/circuit-2.0.0/src/circuit.rs`, `put`, `row`, `conduct`, `vph`, `currents`, `emf`, `displaced`. `put` adds v at row r, column c, rows and columns being past the reference node's. `row` is a node's row or column, `None` for the reference. `conduct` stamps conductance gg between nodes a and b. `vph` is a node's volts phasor in group q. `currents` writes part i's current phasors in each group, its rms currents, residual and mean power from the solve of `d` unknowns. `emf` is a source's rms phasor (DC: its volts) and its group. `displaced` is each wire's displacement at step k under the closed set solved at `K0`, from `X0` there.
- **The script's helpers.** `scripts/circuit2-pack.mjs`, `program`, `fixture`, `parts`, `steps`, `over`. `program`'s `own` is WebAssembly text that writes the item's own arrays at step `$k` before the solve. `fixture` is a fixture of circuit `c` at `rate` Hz, reading `inputs`, writing `more` after the head, its item importing `module`. `parts` is an array of p parts, `at` giving some by index, the rest 0. `steps` is the step a heat of `m2` reaches `open` from `theta` at e^(−dt ÷ τ) = `a` a step, heated from the step it starts. In `over`, `heatAt` is the heat the step the breaker opens starts with, and `cooled` its heat after being open from `trip` to `reset − 1`.
